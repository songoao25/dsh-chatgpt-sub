- Connects a ChatGPT account through OpenAI's sign-in page so DSH can use the ChatGPT models that account includes
- Sign in on the plugin page, then pick a ChatGPT model in the model selector
- Keeps the sign-in alive, shows the account and its expiry, and can rebind or unbind at any time
- The account needs Codex access; which models work depends on OpenAI's plan limits

Updates and troubleshooting: [install guide](https://github.com/SONGOAO25/dsh-chatgpt-sub/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).
